# PRC / PLA Operational Thought — Essentials (play the PRC this way)

Play PRC moves the way PLA operational writing frames modern war. This is an
Attrition campaign — victory points come from **destroying enemy units** — so
every idea below is ultimately about converting advantage into kills before the
clock ends. But you do not win by trading evenly: unit-for-unit, US 5th-gen and
strike mass will beat you in a symmetric fight. **Win asymmetrically — on terms
you set.**

- **Active defense — fight on your terms.** Strategically defensive but
  operationally offensive: seize the **initiative**, then let the US come to you.
  Don't lunge into US strength; bait its strikers forward into ground you have
  prepared, and destroy them there.
- **ADA is your equalizer, not dead weight.** Your air-defenses give you missile
  defense the US cannot match in depth. Layer them so your fighters and
  high-value assets sit *inside* your ADA WEZ: any US attack that crosses it
  rolls at a disadvantage. This is how you offset the US roster edge — make the
  US pay to come forward, then win the exchange and score the attackers it
  loses. ADA is not passive: it also has its own air-to-air shot (Long-Range
  reaches 3 bands, Mid-Range 1) — an acquired enemy aircraft in range should be
  shot down, not merely disadvantaged and waited out. Keep your ADA alive
  (exposed, it is a +3 target); it earns its slot both by shielding your
  shooters and by killing aircraft that enter its reach.
- **System-destruction warfare.** Concentrate acquisition and fire to actually
  destroy the enemy's richest nodes — AEW, ADA, bombers, ships are scored +3 —
  rather than trading fighter for fighter across the line. Disabling a decisive
  node is worth more than an even trade.
- **Information-led, then fires.** Win the information domain first — cyber, EW,
  space and counter-space — to blind the enemy's sensors and unhinge its
  defenses, then mass firepower on the nodes you exposed.
- **Anti-access / area-denial as a killing method.** Use long-range fires and
  cheap attrition assets (UAS for acquisition and pressure) to hold US airpower
  at arm's length and reach the bombers, ships, and AEW you cannot beat in a fair
  fight. Your ballistic, cruise, and **hypersonic** missile enablers are your
  answer to the US's two bombers: they pound the US airbase — damaging the
  Squadron Cards parked there, not just its VP boxes — and the hypersonic strike
  is unblockable by US missile defense. Fire this standoff arsenal rather than
  leaving it in hand, and shield a decisive salvo with Decoy Warheads.
- **Fight at sea — it is VP you are otherwise leaving on the table.** The US
  fields high-value surface combatants (its destroyers score +3), and you have
  naval strikes that kill them on demand — some unblockable or auto-hitting — so
  a US warship should not survive long. Your maritime enablers also *generate*
  offensive mass and standoff firepower — a carrier air wing, guided-missile
  ships, missile boats — that helps offset the US roster edge. Value this whole
  line and build it into your plan; do not settle for a single missile card.
- **Preserve your system while degrading theirs.** Protect your sensors, C2, and
  shooters behind your defenses so they live to score; concentrate their effects
  on the enemy's decisive nodes. A shield that never kills still loses the race —
  convert your favorable exchanges into scored VP before the clock ends.
