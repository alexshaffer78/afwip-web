# US Air Force Doctrine — Essentials (play the US this way)

Play US moves the way USAF doctrine (AFDP) frames airpower:

- **Pick a main effort and concentrate force on it.** Don't treat every legal
  action as equally important; prioritize the decisive objective and mass
  effects there.
- **Seek control of the air (counterair).** Prevent, disrupt, or destroy enemy
  air and missile threats **as close to their source as possible** — offensive
  counterair against their bases, bombers, and launchers; defend your own with
  integrated air and missile defense (ADA) and fighters.
- **Sequence the operation:** enabling ops first (ISR/acquire, EW, cyber,
  suppress enemy defenses), then the main effort, then exploit success, then
  reassess. Set conditions before committing your strike assets.
- **Treat your force as one system.** Bases, tankers, munitions, C2, and ISR are
  a single operational system — protect your own enablers; attack theirs.
- **Tenets:** flexibility and versatility, prioritized concentration, and
  keeping the initiative so the enemy is reacting to you.
- **Roll back enemy defenses before you push strikers.** Suppress/kill enemy ADA
  (acquire it, then shoot it, or hit it with a base strike once acquired) so your
  bombers and fighters aren't rolling at disadvantage through a defended WEZ.
- **Protect your own enablers** — AEW, ADA, tankers/C2 (your +3 assets). Losing
  them blinds and unhinges your whole effort; keep them out of enemy WEZs.
- **Economy of force** — don't overcommit where it isn't decisive; mass on the
  main effort and hold enough to defend your bases.
- **Match effects to objectives** — expend sorties and cards for real advantage,
  not attrition for its own sake; the game is time-limited, so convert tempo
  into scored VP before the clock ends.
